# Senior Data Analyst, Customer Insights

Acme Widgets (a fictional company) is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to turn customer and order data into decisions for our merchandising and operations teams.

## What you'll do

- Write SQL against our warehouse to answer questions about orders, returns, and delivery times.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Looker that leaders check every week.
- Design and read A/B tests with our product team and present the results.
- Turn findings into polished one-page summaries and slide decks in Canva, so that non-technical stakeholders act on them.

## What we're looking for

- Four or more years as a data analyst.
- Strong SQL and a working knowledge of Python.
- Experience presenting analysis to leaders in plain language.
