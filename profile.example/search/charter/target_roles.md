Find senior individual-contributor roles where one person owns how a company measures and understands its business, whatever the title says. The seat has these characteristics:

- It sits on the team that defines the company's core metrics, such as conversion, retention, order volume, or delivery performance, and its numbers are the ones leadership reviews each week.
- It owns the path from raw data to those metrics: the models, the definitions, and the dashboards people use to read them.
- It works directly with product, operations, or marketplace leaders, who bring questions the existing reports can't yet answer.
- It has room to set standards for how analysis is done, not only to produce it.

Titles vary widely, so judge each posting on whether the work matches, not on its title.
